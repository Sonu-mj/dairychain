# Recommendations

> Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED

## Purpose
This file answers what management should do next, in priority order, with each action tied to the evidence that supports it. It is for a manager who needs a short action list, not a wish list.

## In this file
- Priority actions
- What each action needs
- Business impact

## Priority actions
1. Investigate procurement rejection first. Get rejection reasons, fat and SNF, and the breakdown by supplier, district and centre. Rejected volume is 1,117,247.35 L, so even a small fall in the rate matters.
2. Investigate fulfilment second. Get cancellation and partial-delivery reasons by product and day. Orders not delivered in full are 2,834 of 14,000.
3. Get cost inputs before profit decisions. Supply COGS, discounts, returns and operating expenses, then calculate profit by product and period.
4. Get raw milk input per production batch, in the same unit as output, so yield and loss can be calculated.
5. Get the product and daily sales rows, so product and daily decisions use rows, not period totals.

## What each action needs
Each action needs the breakdown named in its Q and A file. A recommendation without its breakdown is a direction, not a completed analysis.

## Business impact
Measured impact: none was supplied, so none is claimed. No profit increase, cost reduction or money saved is reported in this set. Expected benefit: each action above would turn a supplied total into a named cause, and a named cause into a fix that can be measured in the next period.
