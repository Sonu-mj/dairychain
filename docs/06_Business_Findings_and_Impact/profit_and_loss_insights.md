# Profit and Loss Insights


## Purpose
This file answers what can be said about profit and loss from the supplied numbers, which is revenue only. It is for a reader who must not mistake revenue for profit.

## In this file
- Question
- Why it matters
- Data used
- Method
- Result
- What it means
- What to do next

## Question
What does DairyChain show about profit and loss?

## Why it matters
Profit and loss is the final test of a business. Revenue, cost and expense must all be present before that test can be run.

## Data used
Revenue Rs 26,734,294.03 is supplied.

## Method
Net revenue, gross profit and operating profit use the formulas in [../04_Business_Analytics/kpi_definitions.md](../04_Business_Analytics/kpi_definitions.md). Each formula needs its inputs before it gives a result.

## Result
No profit and loss result is reported. No gross profit, operating profit, margin or loss figure was supplied or calculated.

## What it means
The project shows sales revenue, not profit and loss. Any profit and loss statement built from the supplied numbers alone would be invented.

## What to do next
Supply the COGS definition, discount and returns fields, and operating expense records. Then build the profit and loss view by product and by period, and add it here with its query output.
