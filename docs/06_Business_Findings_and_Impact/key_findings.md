## Finding 1: procurement rejection
- Question: how much milk was rejected?
- Result: 2,311 batches rejected of 18,000, 12.84 percent. Rejected volume 1,117,247.35 L of 8,758,507.64 L, 12.76 percent.
- What it means: about 1 in 8 batches and about 1 in 8 litres did not pass quality acceptance.
- Source: user-supplied README, 2026-11-11.

## Finding 2: fulfilment gap
- Question: how much of what was ordered was delivered?
- Result: delivered 11,166 of 14,000 orders, 79.76 percent. Quantity fulfilled 161,036.41 of 181,804, 88.58 percent.
- What it means: 2,834 orders were not delivered in full. The reason is not stated in the totals.
- Source: user-supplied README, 2026-11-11.

## Finding 3: production output by unit
- Question: how much finished product and byproduct was made?
- Result: finished litre-based 1,484,135.25 L, byproduct litre-based 466,514.75 L, finished kg-based 266,416.65 kg, byproduct kg-based 402,413.85 kg, from 4,000 batches.
- What it means: output is known, efficiency is not.
- Source: user-supplied README, 2026-11-11.
