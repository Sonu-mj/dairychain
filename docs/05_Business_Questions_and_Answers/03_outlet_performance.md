## Business question

## Business context
Outlet revenue shows demand at one place. Outlet profit shows whether that demand survives rent, salaries, wastage and returns at that place.

## Analytical approach
When outlet data exists: sum revenue, returns, COGS and operating expenses by outlet, then calculate profit and margin by outlet. Confirm the outlet count from the records first. Do not assume a fixed number.

## KPI definitions
Outlet operating profit equals outlet gross profit minus outlet operating expenses. Outlet margin equals outlet operating profit divided by outlet net revenue, times 100.
