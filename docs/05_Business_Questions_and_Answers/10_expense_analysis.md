# Expense Analysis


## Purpose
It is for a reader who needs cost structure before any operating profit can be trusted.

## In this file
- What are the major expense categories?
- Which expenses consume the largest share of revenue?

## Business question
What are the major expense categories, and which expenses are increasing?

## Business context
A business needs to know where money is spent before it can cut cost safely. Cutting the wrong category can cut the service that earns revenue.

## Data required
No expense categories, amounts, dates or outlet records were named in the README. No expense Gold table is listed.

## Analytical approach
When expense data exists: sum expenses by category and by period, show each category as a share of revenue, and compare growth in expenses with growth in revenue.

## KPI definitions
Expense-to-revenue ratio equals (Operating Expenses / Net Revenue) x 100.


## Direct answer
No expense category, amount or trend can be given for DairyChain.

## Recommendation
Supply expense records with category, amount, date and outlet or department where available.

## Business impact
Measured impact: none. Expected benefit: an expense ranking would show the first cost to investigate.


## Business question
Which outlets have relatively high operating expenses?

## Business context
An outlet with high expenses relative to revenue needs attention even if its sales look strong.


## Analytical approach
Compare operating expenses divided by net revenue, by outlet.

## KPI definitions
Expense-to-revenue ratio by outlet, as defined above.


## Business interpretation
This question is kept here so it is not lost, but its answer is not invented. It depends on the same expense and outlet data as the question above.

## Recommendation
Supply outlet-level expense records with the expense categories.

## Business impact
Measured impact: none. Expected benefit: outlet expense ratios would show which outlets need management attention.
