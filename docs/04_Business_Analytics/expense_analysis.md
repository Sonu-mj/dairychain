## Question
What are the major expense categories, and how much of revenue do operating expenses consume?

## Why it matters
Expenses decide whether revenue becomes profit. Without expense categories and amounts, a manager cannot tell whether rent, salaries, transport, energy or another cost is the main pressure on profit.

## Data used
No expense categories, amounts, dates or outlet records were named in the README. No expense Gold table is listed. Status: NOT IMPLEMENTED in the supplied README scope.

## Method
When expense data exists, the method is: sum expenses by category and by period, divide operating expenses by net revenue for the expense-to-revenue ratio, and compare expense growth with revenue growth. Do not subtract the same cost twice, and do not treat a procurement cost as an operating expense.
