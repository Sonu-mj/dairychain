## Question
Which outlets generate the most revenue and profit, and which outlets need management attention?

## Why it matters
An outlet can have high sales and low profit if rent, salaries, wastage and returns are high. Outlet profit needs revenue, COGS, returns, wastage and operating expenses at outlet level.

## Data used
No outlet table, outlet count, outlet revenue or outlet expense was named in the README. The Gold tables are batch, supplier, centre, product and daily. No outlet Gold table is listed. Status: NOT IMPLEMENTED in the supplied README scope.

## Method
When outlet data exists, the method is: revenue minus returns, minus COGS, minus outlet operating expenses, at outlet level, then margin by outlet. Do not assume a fixed number of outlets. Confirm the count from the records first.

## Result
No outlet result is reported. No outlet name, count, revenue or profit was supplied.

## What it means
No outlet finding can be made for DairyChain from the supplied sources. DairyChain must not borrow outlet details from RetailIQ or any other project.

## What to do next
