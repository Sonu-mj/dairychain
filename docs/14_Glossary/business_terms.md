# Business Terms

> Status: VERIFIED

## Purpose
This file answers what the business words in DairyChain mean, in plain language. It is for a reader who is new to dairy operations and needs the terms before the analysis.

## In this file
- Terms
- How to use this glossary

## Terms
- Procurement: buying and collecting milk from suppliers, through collection centres.
- Collection centre: a place where milk is collected from suppliers before it moves to production.
- Supplier: a person or business that supplies milk to the dairy.
- Quality inspection: checking milk before acceptance, including fat and SNF measurements.
- Fat: a milk quality measure, reported as a percentage or a value in the data.
- SNF: solids-not-fat, a milk quality measure.
- Rejection: milk that is not accepted after inspection, for a recorded reason.
- Production batch: one run of production, with a raw milk input and a finished output.
- Byproduct: a secondary product made during production, in addition to the finished product.
- Fulfilment: delivering what was ordered, in full, in part, or not at all.
- Delivered order: an order delivered in full.
- Partially delivered order: an order delivered in part only.
- Cancelled order: an order that was cancelled and not delivered.

## How to use this glossary
Terms are used with the same meaning in every file. If a term is used differently in a source file, that difference is noted in the file where it appears, and this glossary is updated.
